## Who is not here

- **The Hollow Accord** and **Lantern Cell** are never seen. They are radio fragments and firmware chips. See [The world](../lore/world.md).
- **CHORUS** has no body. It is the voice in the boss banners and the logic behind every enemy. See [CHORUS and the swarms](../lore/chorus.md).
- There are **no human enemies** in the game.
