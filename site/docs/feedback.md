# Send feedback

IRON STATIC is in an open beta. Rough edges, changing numbers and the occasional bug are expected, and your report shapes what gets fixed next.

## Two taps from the game

Open **Settings**, scroll to **ABOUT** and tap **SEND FEEDBACK**. Your browser opens this page. Nothing is sent from the game itself: it makes no network connection and attaches no data to a report.

## The feedback form

Form link: [FEEDBACK FORM URL]

The form asks for the three things below. An email address is not required.

## What to include

1. **The version string.** Settings > ABOUT shows it, for example `VERSION 0.5.0-beta.1 (50001)`. It tells us exactly which build you have.
2. **Your phone.** Make and model, and the Android version if you know it.
3. **What happened.** What you were doing, what you expected, what you saw. For a number that looks wrong, add the wave, your frame and the upgrades you had.

Useful extras: whether it happens every time, and whether it happened in a TIMED or a PURGE run.

## What happens next

Reports are sorted into crashes, wrong numbers, confusing screens and wishes. Crashes and lost progress come first. See [Status](status.md) for what is built and what is planned.
