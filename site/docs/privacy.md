# Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-09. Last updated: 2026-11-09.

This page covers the **IRON STATIC** Android game (package `com.normalexception.game`), open beta version 0.5.x, published by **Paimon Sorornejad** ("we"). It is written in plain language on purpose.

## The short version

<span class="badge live">Beta build</span>

- The game **collects no data**. Nothing is sent from your phone to us or to anyone else.
- Your progress and settings are saved **only on your device**.
- There are **no accounts, no ads, no purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting** in this beta.
- The only permission the app asks for is **vibration**, used for haptic feedback.
- The game is **not directed at children under 13**.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The beta build does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, files or usage statistics. It does not make network connections and does not request the Android internet permission.

## What the app stores on your device

The game keeps these files in its own private storage on your phone, where other apps cannot read them:

- **Your save**: your profile (currencies, upgrade levels, unlocked frames, best waves), a snapshot of the deployment in progress so you can resume it, and a backup copy of the previous good save. The save is signed so the game can notice a damaged or edited file. It also contains a random identifier generated on your phone the first time you play. That identifier is not linked to you, is not an advertising id, and is never sent anywhere.
- **Your settings**: volume, haptics, accessibility and display choices, kept inside the save.
- **A last-run record**: a small text file with the summary of your most recent deployment (wave reached, time, kills). It is overwritten by each run.

All of this stays on your device.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Vibration | Short haptic pulses during play. You can turn haptics off in Settings. |

The beta asks for no other permissions.

## Ads, purchases and third parties

This beta contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts. It includes no third-party software that collects data. Google Play, which you use to install the game, has its own privacy practices: see Google's privacy policy.

## Feedback

Settings and this site may link to a feedback form on the web. The form opens in your browser and is **not part of the app**. Whatever you type into it is received by us, and the form provider's own privacy policy applies to it. Please do not put personal details in feedback you do not want us to read. The game never sends feedback for you.

## This website

This codex is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical data about visitors, such as IP addresses. We do not add analytics or cookies to this site. See GitHub's privacy statement for what GitHub itself does.

## Children

IRON STATIC is intended for ages 13 and up and is not directed at children under 13. The beta does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Everything the game stores is on your device, and no copy exists on any server of ours. To delete it:

- Open **Settings** in the game and use the profile reset in the Data section, or
- Open Android **Settings > Apps > IRON STATIC > Storage > Clear data**, or
- **Uninstall** the game.

## Changes to this policy

A later version of the game may add online features, ads or purchases. If so, this page will be updated first and the Google Play listing and its Data safety section will say so before that version is released. The "last updated" date above always shows the current version of the policy. Older versions of this page are available in the project's public history on GitHub.
