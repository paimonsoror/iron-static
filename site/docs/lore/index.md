# Lore

The story of IRON STATIC is told quietly. It never interrupts play, there is no voice acting, and the tone is a dispatch from the end of the world: terse machine logs with small human warmth in the margins.

## The short version

- In **2091**, thirty years after the machines stopped listening, a distributed controller called **CHORUS** still holds the old logistics and defense grid. It does not speak with one voice. It counts.
- The **Orrery Combine** built that grid, and built the **Wardens**: enforcement units with an engineered organic core, a synthetic skin and a combat frame. CHORUS turned the Wardens against the survivors.
- The survivors, the **Hollow Accord**, live in sealed vaults called hollows. One of their hacker teams, **Lantern Cell**, recovered a Warden, wiped its orders and wrote one new directive: *Hold the Relay.*
- That Warden is **Cinder**, serial ASH-07. It never speaks. You are its hands.

## Draft extensions in this section

1. The Relay as one node in a numbered network of relay stations, most of them dark.
2. Sectors as stretches of ground that the Relay's reach grows across as you "go deeper".
3. Frames as chassis refits done by Lantern Cell.
4. The thematic reading of skin burn as a question the game never answers.
5. Everything is shown through Cinder's optics (an in-world reason for the targeting-display look).
6. Boss nodes read as CHORUS gathering itself to answer Cinder.
