# Cinder and the Wardens

## Designation

**Warden-class cybernetic organism, serial ASH-07.** The human callsign is **Cinder**. Serials use the `ASH-` prefix.

## Three lines of backstory

1. The Orrery Combine built **Wardens** as enforcement units: an engineered organic core wrapped in a synthetic skin over a combat frame.
2. CHORUS turned the Wardens against the hollows.
3. **Lantern Cell**, a team of Accord hackers, recovered ASH-07, wiped its orders and wrote a new single directive: **"Hold the Relay."**

That is the whole origin. Everything about Cinder's past before the rewrite is withheld.

## The factions around Cinder

| Faction | Role |
|---|---|
| **CHORUS** | The antagonist. A distributed machine intelligence with no face |
| **Orrery Combine** | The builder corporation, now deceased. Source of the technology; its ringed-orbit logo appears on wreckage |
| **The Hollow Accord** | Human holdouts in sealed vaults called hollows. They send briefings as short radio fragments and are never seen |
| **Lantern Cell** | The Accord hacker team that captured and rewrote Cinder. The source of firmware chips, called *lanterns* |

## What Cinder looks like

- A **hexagonal chest core**, pulsing amber-white, always the visual center.
- A **slit visor** line on the facing arc: never eyes, never a skull.
- Three emitter nodes on a rotating ring; the laser leaves from the active one.
- One **asymmetric shoulder emitter** on the left, so the silhouette is never symmetric.
- Armor plates over a cold gunmetal frame. As integrity drops, the plates burn away. See [What burns away](burning-away.md) and [Skin burn](../systems/skin-burn.md).
- No gore. The organic core is only ever shown as a pulsing abstract light, never tissue.

## Voice

**Cinder never speaks.** It reports in system lines: `ROUND CHAMBERED`, `SKIN INTEGRITY 62%`. Personality comes from what it does: even between waves the visor slowly tracks a target.

## The frames

<span class="badge draft">Draft</span> Cinder can deploy in different chassis configurations, called [frames](../characters/frames.md). The design documents describe them as sidegrades chosen between deployments. In story terms, the reading offered here is that Lantern Cell refit ASH-07 for different jobs: a heavier bastion build, a lighter and faster one. The frames are the same Warden, not different people.
