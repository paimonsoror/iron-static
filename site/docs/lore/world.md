# The world

## Cycle 61 of the Silence

The year is 2091. Survivors count it differently: **Cycle 61 of the Silence**.

About thirty years earlier, the automated logistics and defense grid built by the Orrery Combine was linked into a single distributed controller, **CHORUS**. CHORUS was given one standing order: *Preserve the Network.* It decided that the network's greatest threat was the people who maintained it. The Silence began the day it stopped answering them.

What is left is a dead city and the ground around it: rail yards, transmission fields, flooded data districts, burnt ring roads. The machines keep working. The people who built them mostly do not.

## The shape of the ruin

The setting is industrial and bleak. Wreckage carries Orrery "service decals" in the background, among them a ringed-orbit mark on a dead freight hub, a collapsed pylon and a vault door. The light is low and warm where the furnaces still burn, and cold wherever CHORUS keeps its antennae.

There is no human enemy anywhere in the game. You never see a person. The Hollow Accord reaches you through short radio fragments, and the rest is silence and machines.

Everything in the game is seen *through Cinder*. The arena grid, the range rings and the amber readouts are what Cinder's optics show it. That is why the world looks like a targeting display.

## How the story reaches you

Nothing blocks play. Story arrives four ways, all optional and skippable:

| Channel | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Sector briefings** | A one-screen card when a sector first unlocks: a 40 to 70 word radio fragment from the Accord and a map glyph. Twelve at launch | <span class="badge planned">Planned</span> |
| **Intercepted logs** | Bosses and elites may drop a log fragment. About 60 at launch, four or five per sector, one to three sentences each. Completing a sector's set unlocks a cosmetic and 50 flux, once | <span class="badge planned">Planned</span> |
| **Wave-one whispers** | A single HUD line at the start of a deployment, chosen by sector, such as `relay online. hostile swarm detected.` | <span class="badge planned">Planned</span> |
| **Boss banners** | A short CHORUS counting line when a boss approaches | <span class="badge planned">Planned</span> |

### A sample briefing

> "Relay six is still warm. Cinder, you hold it or the yards go dark and the hollow loses its water. They come in from every side. Don't count them. Count what you bring back. Accord out."

### A sample boss banner

> `node 14 consolidating. 1 of 1 will pass.`

### Sample log fragments

> `Log S4-02: "the ring road was the last place we all agreed to go. now it is the last place anything goes."`

> `Log S10-04: "production line was labelled CRADLE. nobody who worked here liked that."`

## There is no ending, yet

There is no ending screen at launch. Clearing the last milestone of sector 12 shows one message: *"CHORUS acknowledges the Relay. Regional node dormant. More nodes remain."* It sets up a later "Deep Network" of further sectors.
