## The Relay

**The Relay** is the diegetic name for the arena center: the last working relay of the dead city, and the thing every enemy is walking toward. Cinder is bolted to it. The Accord's first briefing refers to it by number: "relay six", still warm, and a hollow whose water depends on it.

In play, the Relay is the circle you defend. The arena has a 52 meter radius, enemies spawn on its edge, and Cinder never moves.

<span class="badge draft">Draft</span> *Relay six* implies a numbered network. The reading offered here is that most of the other relays have gone dark, which is why this one matters and why "hold" is the only order Cinder has.

## Deployments

One attempt at holding the Relay is a **deployment**. It begins at wave 1 and ends when Cinder's integrity reaches zero. What Cinder recovers during the deployment (data) is banked, and the next deployment starts from a stronger position. This is what "go deeper every time" means.

## The twelve sectors

A **sector** is a rung on the difficulty ladder. Twelve ship at launch; only Sector 1 is playable today.

| # | Sector | Setting | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Quiet Yards | Rail yards of a dead freight hub | Hulk |
| 2 | Pylon Fields | Collapsed transmission arrays | Hive |
| 3 | Glasswater | Flooded data-center district, reflective floors | Lance |
| 4 | Ashfall Ring | Burnt orbital road around a vanished city | Null |
| 5 | Hollow Gate | Sealed vault entrance, Accord hollow ahead | Hulk |
| 6 | Furnace Mile | Orrery foundry district, hot light | Hive |
| 7 | Static Orchard | Antenna groves, CHORUS relay forest | Lance |
| 8 | The Long Convoy | Stalled machine convoy highway | Null |
| 9 | Salt Bridge | Collapsed span over a dry sea | Hulk |
| 10 | Cradle Works | Warden production line, Cinder's origin | Hive |
| 11 | Choir Hall | CHORUS regional core, mirrored walls | Lance |
| 12 | Orrery Seat | Combine headquarters, final regional node | Null |

Each sector adds enemy levels on top of the last (a +25 level offset per sector) and pays larger rewards. <span class="badge draft">Draft</span> In story terms the sectors are ground the Relay's reach grows across: the further you push, the closer you get to where the machines were made.

Sector 10 is the **Cradle Works**, the Warden production line where Cinder began. Sector 12 is the **Orrery Seat**, the Combine's own headquarters.
