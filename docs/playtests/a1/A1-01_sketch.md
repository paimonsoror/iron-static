2. Is `sfx_laser_shot` tolerable when repeated 20+ times per second (try looping it)?
3. Weight of `sfx_boss_warn`, `sfx_death`, `sfx_enemy_death_heavy`.
4. Is the music too busy or too quiet; any sound to cut or add?
