# The Tower - Idle Tower Defense: Permanent Meta-Progression Systems

Research limits: fandom and game-vault wikis returned 402/403 to fetch, and the official Notion wiki (the-tower.notion.site) renders with JavaScript and returned empty. Findings come from search-result summaries and a few fetchable guide sites (MuMuPlayer, OSLink, BlueStacks, towerstats.org index, tower-hub index). Most per-level cost tables could not be retrieved. Dates are mostly 2025; nothing was verified as late-2026 state.

## Workshop (permanent coin upgrades, plus enhancement)

### Takeaway
The Workshop is a coin-funded permanent upgrade screen that mirrors in-run upgrades (attack, defense, utility, and a weapons tab), extended by "Workshop Enhancements" that are unlocked and discounted through Labs. Exact cost curves were not retrievable.

### Cited Findings
- Early guidance describes the Workshop as spending coins on Base Damage, Attack Speed, Critical Chance and Critical Factor, with Damage first; crit upgrades are "significantly more expensive" early. — [BlueStacks beginners guide](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/the-tower-idle-tower-defense/ttitd-beginners-guide-en.html) (undated, simplified)
- The Workshop has 48 upgrades (each with an unlock cost, max level, max value, coins to max), plus the Wall (6 labs) and 18 Workshop Enhancements with costs and bonuses. — [towerstats.org guide index](https://towerstats.org/guide/)
- Ultimate weapons are the fourth Workshop tab per one guide (unverified). — [search summary, OSLink/wiki](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-beginner-guide.html)
- Workshop damage upgrades are boosted by cards, relics, modules and Workshop Enhancements; the wiki Workshop page is about 740 days old. — [game-vault Workshop](https://the-tower-idle-tower-defense.game-vault.net/wiki/Workshop) (search summary)
- Labs reduce Workshop costs: Workshop Attack/Defense/Utility Discount, Workshop Respect, Workshop Enhancement, and Enhancement Coin Discount researches exist. — [MuMuPlayer lab guide](https://www.mumuplayer.com/blog/the-tower-lab-research-guide.html)
- Dissonant Runs "lock one workshop tab" in exchange for a permanent per-tier boost (up to 5x damage, health or UW damage; up to 3x coins; plus Echo). — [tower-hub wiki index](https://www.tower-hub.com/wiki/system)

### Inferences
- Design pattern: each in-run upgrade has a permanent counterpart in the Workshop that sets the base value; an enhancement layer on top gives a second coin sink once base upgrades stall.

### Gaps
- Per-upgrade coin costs, cost scaling formula, enhancement list and unlock thresholds were not retrievable (wiki pages blocked).

## Labs (timed research)

### Takeaway
Labs are timed, coin-funded research slots: 1 free slot plus gem-bought slots (total 5 or 6 depending on source). Research duration, not coins, is the main bottleneck; speed-ups use gems or Elite Cells.

### Cited Findings
- Labs unlock early by completing Milestones at Tier 1 Wave 30; 1 free slot, more bought with gems: 2nd 100, 3rd 400, 4th 1,400, 5th 3,000 (total 4,900). — [MuMuPlayer](https://www.mumuplayer.com/blog/the-tower-lab-research-guide.html); total 4,900 corroborated by [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/). Slot-count conflict: MuMuPlayer says "Five more can be bought", while [OSLink](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-beginner-guide.html) says "up to five" total and its own sibling guide says start with one, up to five.
- Research categories (MuMuPlayer): Main (Game Speed, Starting Cash, Workshop discounts, Labs Coin Discount, Labs Speed, Buy Multiplier, More Round Stats, Target Priority, Card Presets, Reroll Daily Mission, Workshop Enhancement); Attack (Damage, Attack Speed, Crit Factor, Range, Damage Per Meter, Super Crit, Rend Armor, Light Speed Shots); Defense (Health, Regen, Defense Abs/%, Orb Speed, Land Mine, Shockwave, six Wall researches, Garlic Thorns); Utility (Cash Bonus, Cash Per Wave, Coins/Kill, Interest, Max Interest, Package After Boss, Recovery Package, Enemy Attack Level Skip); Ultimate Weapon (per-weapon labs); Card (extra effects, recharge, Card Mastery Labs); Perk (Unlock Perks, Waves Required, Auto Pick, Standard Perk Bonus, First Perk Choice, Trade-Off improve); Bot (cooldown/duration per bot); Enemies (health/attack per enemy type, boss, protector); Module (drop chances, shard costs, unmerge, shatter, effect bans, assist module labs); Battle Condition (4 groups). — [MuMuPlayer](https://www.mumuplayer.com/blog/the-tower-lab-research-guide.html)
- Speed-ups: gem rush instantly finishes remaining time (longer research = better value); Elite Cell "lab boosting" with chosen multiplier and duration; roughly 1.5x across all labs advised as efficient; auto-boost exists. Switching labs refunds coins and keeps progress; Auto Research, Lab Search, Lab History exist. — [MuMuPlayer](https://www.mumuplayer.com/blog/the-tower-lab-research-guide.html)
- Game-speed pricing example (may not be lab pricing): 1.5x for 1 hour costs 15 gems, 2x costs 80 gems/hour. — [OSLink workshop/lab guide](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-workshop-upgrade-guide.html)
- Rough gem rush estimate of about 150 gems for a 24-hour lab (unverified community estimate). — [Notion gem guide, search summary](https://the-tower.notion.site/Guide-to-Gems-Gem-Spending-1c091383b93f80e1a30df6cdb8b790c3)
- Priorities: Slot 1 Game Speed first (cap 5x), then Attack Speed (about level 50); Slot 2 Lab Speed once labs take days; Slots 3-4 economy (Coins/Kill, Interest, Cash per Wave); avoid Range; deprioritize Health Regen/Absolute Defense; start long labs before sleep. — [OSLink beginner guide](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-beginner-guide.html), [OSLink lab guide](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-workshop-upgrade-guide.html)
- Coin cost rises with level but coins are easy to farm; duration is the bottleneck; later labs take days. — [OSLink lab guide](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-workshop-upgrade-guide.html)

### Inferences
- Labs are the main time-gate: parallel slots bought with a steeply rising gem price (about 4x then 2x then 2x), timers growing to days, and accelerators that are premium-adjacent. Meta-researches (game speed, lab speed, discounts) are done first because they compound.

### Gaps
- Per-research timers, coin costs, max levels, the timer scaling formula and exact speed-up price tables were not found.

## Ultimate Weapons

### Takeaway
Nine weapons bought with Power Stones at a rising price by number owned (9,705 stones for all), chosen from random offers; each has three upgradable stats, with a "plus" tier after owning all.

### Cited Findings
- Cost by weapon number: 5, 50, 150, 300, 800, 1,250, 1,750, 2,400, 3,000 stones (sum 9,705). Three random offers per purchase, equal odds, offers persist until bought. — [game-vault UW guide](https://the-tower-idle-tower-defense.game-vault.net/wiki/Guide:Ultimate_Weapons_Sync) (via fetch summary); total corroborated by [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/). An older/other source says a choice between two. — search summary
- Stones come from tournaments (one source says only tournaments); tournaments are held about twice a week and give gems, stones, keys. — [OSLink](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-beginner-guide.html)
- Weapons: Golden Tower (coin boost), Black Hole (multiplies coins with Golden Tower; timing matters), Spotlight (damage amp plus coin bonus), Death Wave (coin, health, damage modifiers; core to Devo builds), Chain Lightning (no cooldown, applies Shock), Smart Missiles (boosts Spotlight missiles), Inner Land Mines (stun), Poison Swamp (stun, Rend amplifies damage), Chrono Field (needs near 100% uptime via heavy investment). Suggested order Golden Tower, Black Hole, Spotlight, Death Wave. — game-vault guide above; [OSLink](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-beginner-guide.html)
- Weapons auto-fire when cooldown resets; toggling resets the cooldown and is limited to 40 toggles per run. Damage is a percent of tower damage including crit. — same sources
- UW+ abilities unlock only after all 9 are owned; UW+ stone costs exist (not retrieved). Labs for a weapon unlock per owned weapon (one guide: tier 7). — [search summary](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/the-tower-idle-tower-defense/ttitd-weapons-guide-en.html); [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/)
- Synergies: Golden Tower + Black Hole (cooldown sync), Smart Missiles + Spotlight, Inner Land Mines + Smart Missiles, Chain Lightning + Shock and Dimensional Core module. — OSLink guide above

### Inferences
- Pricing is by count, not by weapon, so random offers make early order partly luck; the first purchase at 5 stones is a tutorial-cheap hook.

### Gaps
- Per-weapon upgrade costs, UW+ costs and base numbers.

## Cards

### Takeaway
Cards are gem-bought collectibles leveled by star (1-7) via duplicate copies, equipped in gem-purchased slots; a late Card Mastery layer costs Stones and is lab-upgradable.

### Cited Findings
- 32 cards with level 1-7 values; towerstats tracks gem cost per slot, copies per star and drop rates by rarity (figures not retrieved). — [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/)
- Cards are bought with gems; gems spawn in runs (every 10-15 minutes per one source, about 3 minutes per another: conflict); about 400 gems/day from missions plus gem farming. — [OSLink beginner](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-beginner-guide.html) vs [OSLink card guide](https://www.oslink.io/blog/guide/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-card-tier-list-and-guide.html)
- Example cards: Energy Shield, Nuke, Second Wind, Demon Mode, Critical Coin, Death Ray; build cards such as Fortress and extra orbs. — [theTowerStats](https://mahbam42.github.io/theTowerStats/cards), [OSLink](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-beginner-guide.html)
- Slot guidance: unlock labs first, delay slot spending, 5-6 slots before modules; wait for commons near 5-star and 10+ slots before heavy modules. — [Notion gem guide, search summary](https://the-tower.notion.site/Guide-to-Gems-Gem-Spending-1c091383b93f80e1a30df6cdb8b790c3)
- Presets: unlocked by the Lab research "Card Presets"; 5 presets per a third-party tracker (unconfirmed). — [theTowerStats](https://mahbam42.github.io/theTowerStats/cards)
- Card Mastery: unlock by maxing all cards to 7 stars and reaching Wave 100 on Tier 16; costs Stones for first level, then lab upgrades. — [Notion Card Mastery, search summary](https://the-tower.notion.site/Card-Mastery-1c291383b93f8092b596d4c9c015918e)

### Gaps
- Slot gem costs, max slots, rarity tiers, copies per star, drop rates.

## Modules

### Takeaway
Four slot types (cannon, armor, generator, core), a rarity ladder to 5-star Ancestral (level 300), 27 unique effects, merge/shatter/shard systems; deliberately a late-game system.

### Cited Findings
- Rarity ladder up to 5-star Ancestral (level 300); 27 unique effects with sub-effect values and reroll costs. — [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/)
- Rare modules can merge up to Legendary+; Epic can reach Ancestral 5-star; commons cannot merge; effects have their own rarity capped by the module rarity. — [r/TheTowerGame post, via search](https://axebps-redlib.hf.space/r/TheTowerGame/comments/1j85sbj/all_about_modules_some_math/mh487xh/?context=3)
- Shatter gives shards: 5 per common, 10 per rare; four shard shapes level matching modules. — [Japanese wiki](https://w.atwiki.jp/the_tower/pages/35.html), search summary
- Boss drops give commons/rares (rare from 0.5%); epic and higher are not from drops. — same
- Module labs: drop chances, reroll shards, shard/coin cost, unmerge, shatter, effect bans. — [MuMuPlayer](https://www.mumuplayer.com/blog/the-tower-lab-research-guide.html)
- Advice: use boss-dropped rares until all labs and 5-6+ card slots. — Notion gem guide above

### Gaps
- Pull rates, substat roll rules, merge counts, module unlock tier.

## Perks, Bots, Guardian, Relics, Vault, Milestones/Tiers, Achievements, Themes

### Takeaway
Each is a separate currency-gated layer: Bots (event medals), Guardian (guild Bits/tokens), Relics (medals/tokens/missions), Vault (keys), tiers/milestones (wave goals), themes (cosmetic with passive coin bonus).

### Cited Findings
- Perks: 34 total (15 standard, 9 UW, 10 trade-off); offer odds 65/20/15%; wave requirements start at wave 200; 9 perk labs; Lab "Unlock Perks". — [tower-hub](https://www.tower-hub.com/wiki/system), [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/)
- Bots: 5 bots (Flame, Thunder, Golden, Amplify, plus one more), bought with event medals at escalating prices 150/300/600/900; persist across events; respec 300 gems once per event; suggested order Golden, Amplify, Thunder, Flame. Stats: Flame 75s cooldown; Thunder 5s stun 120s; Golden x2 coins 120s; Amplify x3.5 damage 120s. Bot+ abilities cost 1,250 stones. — [game-vault Events, search summary](https://the-tower-idle-tower-defense.game-vault.net/wiki/Events), [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/)
- Guardian: guild-only, unlock 200 Bits, 6 chips (Attack, Ally, Bounty, Fetch, Summon, Scout), slots 200/300 Bits, extra chips 200 guild tokens, respec 150 gems, about 83,455 Bits to max everything; added in v26 (Mar 2025). — [Fandom Guardian](https://the-tower-idle-tower-defense.fandom.com/wiki/Guardian) (search summary), [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/), [OSLink v26](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-v26-update.html)
- Guilds unlock at Tier 3 Wave 10; 30 members, 8-week seasons, weekly chests. — [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/)
- Relics: 313 total; event relics 350/700 medals (older: 200/500), guild relics 75/150 tokens; profile shows 5 equipped relics; one relic tied to Tier 18 Wave 1000. — [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/), [tower-hub](https://www.tower-hub.com/wiki/system)
- Vault (v29): Power (47 upgrades), Harmony, Enemy (30 levels) trees; 73,022 keys total. Power Tree tier 2 needs 15 tier-1 nodes, tier 3 needs 25 tier-1 plus 15 tier-2. — [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/), [Notion Power Tree, search summary](https://the-tower.notion.site/Power-Tree-1c691383b93f807aae2fe6d5b39b2ef0)
- Tiers: 24 tiers, coin bonus x1.0 to x103; milestone rewards per tier; events unlock at Tier 1 Wave 70 and run about 2 weeks. — [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/), [game-vault Event guide](https://the-tower-idle-tower-defense.game-vault.net/wiki/Guide:Event_Guide)
- Themes give passive coin bonuses. — [tower-hub](https://www.tower-hub.com/wiki/system)

### Gaps
- Achievements not confirmed as a system; Vault key sources; milestone reward details.

## Progression timeline and walls

### Takeaway
Early game is Game Speed and Attack labs (labs open at Tier 1 Wave 30); gems fund cards then lab slots; stones (tournaments) fund UWs; modules, mastery, vault and bots are months-long sinks.

### Cited Findings
- Gate points: waves 20-25 open deeper features ([BlueStacks](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/game-guides/the-tower-idle-tower-defense/ttitd-beginners-guide-en.html)); labs at T1 W30; events T1 W70; guilds T3 W10; perks need wave 200+; Card Mastery T16 W100.
- Farming about 800 waves per 8 hours at 1x. — [OSLink](https://www.oslink.io/blog/the-tower-idle-tower-defense-beginner-guide.html)
- Sink sizes: 4,900 gems (labs), 9,705 stones (UWs), 83,455 Bits (Guardian), 73,022 keys (Vault), about 400 gems/day income. — [towerstats.org](https://towerstats.org/guide/), OSLink

### Inferences
- Walls: lab timers (days), gem scarcity, tournament-gated stones/keys, medal-gated bots. Many layered sinks in different currencies with different cadences keep long-term goals always available. The day/week/month timeline is inferred, not sourced.

### Gaps
- No sourced day/week/month/year timeline; late-2026 patch changes not covered.
